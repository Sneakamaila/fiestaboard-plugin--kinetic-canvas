A novel, synaesthetic generative art and acoustic soundscape plugin for **Vestaboard** and **FiestaBoard**.

While split-flap displays are conventionally treated as static text signboards, **Kinetic Canvas** embraces the Vestaboard as a **kinetic acoustic instrument** (*Klangskulptur*). Every visual transition is algorithmically composed alongside its physical mechanical sound.

---

## 📐 The Acoustic Physics of the Vestaboard

Every module (*bit*) of a Vestaboard (Note or Flagship) contains a physical spool with **exactly 64 die-cut flaps**:
* **Flap 0:** Blank / Black background (`{0}` / space `" "`)
* **Flaps 1–26:** Uppercase letters `A` through `Z`
* **Flaps 27–36:** Digits `1` through `9`, and `0`
* **Flaps 37–56:** 20 Punctuation marks & Icons (`!`, `@`, `#`, `$`, `(`, `)`, `-`, `+`, `&`, `=`, `;`, `:`, `'`, `"`, `%`, `,`, `.`, `/`, `?`, `°` / `❤️`)
* **Flaps 57–63:** 7 Physical color chips (`{63}` Poppy Red, `{64}` Orange, `{65}` Yellow, `{66}` Green, `{67}` Blue, `{68}` Violet, `{69}` White)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Die 64-Klappen-Geometrie:** Nach Weiß (`{69}`) folgt mechanisch unmittelbar die Blank-/Schwarz-Klappe (`{0}` / ` `), gefolgt von `A` (`{1}`).  
> Der Übergang **Weiß (`69`) $\to$ Schwarz/Blank (`0`) ist exakt 1 Klappen-Schritt ($\Delta = 1$, 35 ms)**!  
> Code `{70}` (Schwarz auf weißem Board) ist auf schwarzen Boards kein separates 65. Klappenblatt, sondern ein Alias für Klappe 0.

Because the stepper motors rotate **strictly forward in one direction**, the mechanical sound generated by any transition is a direct function of the drum travel distance:

$$\Delta = (\text{Target} - \text{Start}) \pmod{64}$$

* **Pianissimo / Staccato Snap ($\Delta = 1$):**  
  Advancing adjacent flaps produces a single, dry mechanical click (~35 ms). If all modules advance in unison, the board emits a crisp mechanical whip-crack followed by instant silence.
* **Ewiges Kontinuum:**  
  Ein geschlossener kinetischer Kreis über alle 64 Klappen. Kein Reset, keine Neuausrichtung — alle Module klicken bei jedem Intervall zeitgleich um 1 Klappe weiter.
* **Acoustic Wave & Spatial Pan:**  
  By scaling $\Delta$ along the columns, sound sweeps laterally across the display.
* **Harmonic Resonance / Singing Bowl:**  
  Assigning large step distances to the center and short distances to the perimeter creates a mechanical strike that decays like a temple gong.
* **Fortissimo / Thunder ($\Delta \approx 60$):**  
  Full cyclic rotations generate a rolling, thunderous mechanical roar lasting over 2 seconds.

## ✨ Generative Art & Sound Engines

### 1. 🟥 Mondrian & De Stijl
* **Visual:** Generative asymmetrical grid compositions inspired by Piet Mondrian. Black dividing lines (`{70}`) framing pure primary color fields (Poppy Red `{63}`, Cobalt Blue `{67}`, Sun Yellow `{65}`, and Canvas White `{69}`).
* **Acoustics:** Bold geometric shifts producing crisp, well-structured acoustic swells.

### 2. 🧬 Conway's Game of Life
* **Visual:** Toroidal cellular automata simulation adapted for 22×6 (Flagship) and 15×3 (Note). Cell age is mapped dynamically to vivid split-flap colors (Newborn = Yellow `{65}`, Youth = Green `{66}`, Mature = Blue `{67}`, Elder = Violet `{68}`).
* **Acoustics:** Every generation reflects the biological birth/death rate. Extinctions and stable oscillators whisper; population blooms trigger dense mechanical cascades. Automatically reseeds if stagnant.

### 3. 📜 Wolfram 1D Automata (Rule 30, 90, 110, 184)
* **Visual:** Stephen Wolfram's legendary elementary cellular automata, continuously scrolling downward across the board like an ancient kinetic tapestry.
* **Acoustics:** Rhythmic, rolling step pulses on every scroll generation.

### 4. 🌦️ Weather Resonance (Live Ambient Weather)
* **Visual:** Connects to the free global Open-Meteo API. Maps live temperature, wind speed, precipitation, and cloud cover to atmospheric color fields:
  * **Rain:** Slanted kinetic streaks of Blue `{67}` and White `{69}`.
  * **Sun:** Warm radiant cores of Yellow `{65}` and Orange `{64}`.
  * **Thunderstorm:** Electric high-contrast fissures.
  * **Snow:** Gentle, crystalline patterns.
* **Acoustics:** Wind speed modulates column turbulence; precipitation rate modulates mechanical droplet frequency. Includes an instant demo/offline fallback.

### 5. 🔔 Zen Chime & Sound Sculptures
* **Pure Abstract Color Art:** Every sound sculpture generates pure geometric color compositions (Bauhaus concentric frames, diagonal prisms, minimalist night drizzle, sacred mandalas) using the physical color chips (`{63}` through `{70}`). Zero plain letters or wall of text.
* **Direct Modes / Sound Envelopes:**
  * `zen_snap`: **Staccato Snap ($\Delta = 1$):** Pure Bauhaus color frames advancing synchronously in the drum's contiguous color ring. All 132 modules click in unison (~35 ms) like a mechanical whip-crack.
  * `zen_wave`: **Klangwelle:** Flowing diagonal rainbow prism with acoustic stereo-sweep from left to right.
  * `zen_rain`: **Nachtregen:** Minimalist night sky on deep Black (`{70}`) with Blue (`{67}`) and White (`{69}`) droplets clicking softly like rain against glass.
  * `zen_gong`: **Resonanz-Mandala:** Radiating color mandala where outer edges stay silent while the core resonates.

### 6. 🔄 Exhibition Cycle (`cycle`)
* Automatically rotates through all art and acoustic styles on every refresh interval.

## ⚡ Wichtige Einstellungen fuer den echten Staccato Snap (Hardware-Unisono)

Damit alle 132 Klappen beim Staccato Snap zeitgleich in einem einzigen mechanischen Peitschenschlag (~35 ms) schalten statt nacheinander als Welle abzulaufen:

1. **In FiestaBoard (Page Editor):**
   * Oeffne deine Kinetic Canvas Seite im FiestaBoard Page Editor.
   * Waehle in der oberen Symbolleiste im Dropdown **Transition** die Option **`None`** (bzw. Keine Animation).
   * *Hintergrund:* Standardmaessig steht FiestaBoard auf `Wave` oder `Use global default`, wodurch FiestaBoard die Klappen kuenstlich Spalte fuer Spalte verzoegert. `None` sendet die Matrix direkt ohne Software-Verzoegerung an das Board.

2. **In der Vestaboard-Hardware (Vestaboard App / Cloud):**
   * Stelle in den Vestaboard-App-Einstellungen unter *Transitions* den Modus auf **`Classic`** (Speed: `Fast`).
   * *Hintergrund:* Im Werksmodus `Wave` oder `Gentle` verzoegert auch die Vestaboard-Firmware die Spalten intern. Im Modus `Classic` drehen sich laut offizieller Vestaboard-Dokumentation alle Klappen **vollstaendig simultan im selben Moment**.

## ⚙️ Configuration Reference

| Setting | Type | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mode` | string | `"cycle"` | `"cycle"`, `"zen_snap"`, `"zen_wave"`, `"zen_rain"`, `"zen_gong"`, `"mondrian"`, `"game_of_life"`, `"wolfram"`, `"weather"`, `"snap_calibration"` |
| `display_layout` | string | `"full_canvas"` | `"full_canvas"` (100% art) or `"canvas_with_info"` (art + title & sound waveform) |
| `refresh_seconds` | integer | `30` | Refresh interval in seconds (minimum 30s to preserve hardware) |
| `snap_pattern` | string | `"continuum"` | [Nur bei Staccato Snap] `"continuum"` (Endlos-Snap ohne Reset), `"bauhaus_frames"`, `"chromatic_chevrons"`, `"heart_bloom"`, `"color_pillars"` |
| `wolfram_rule` | integer | `30` | [Nur bei Wolfram] `30` (Chaotisch), `90` (Sierpinski), `110` (Komplex), `184` (Verkehr) |
| `city_name` | string | `"Berlin"` | [Nur bei Wetter] Anzeigename des Standorts |
| `latitude` | number | `52.52` | [Nur bei Wetter] Breitengrad für Open-Meteo |
| `longitude` | number | `13.405` | [Nur bei Wetter] Längengrad für Open-Meteo |
| `demo_mode` | boolean | `false` | [Nur bei Wetter] Offline / Demo-Modus ohne Netzwerk |
| `seed` | integer | `0` | [Erweitert] Fester Zufallswert (0 = dynamisch) |

> **Night Mode & Quiet Hours:** Scheduling and muting during sleep hours is handled natively by FiestaBoard's global board scheduler.
